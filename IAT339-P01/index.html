Branding Idea

About Us

QQ Tea is an affordable bubble tea shop that offers a variety of customizable drinks with convenient in-store pickup. Customers can order ahead and choose to pick up their drinks as soon as possible or schedule a pickup time. The brand is meant to be friendly, casual, and approachable, like a friend who helps you get your favourite drink without spending too much money or time. We believe our affordable pricing and order-ahead feature make the shop appealing to customers who want a quick and budget-friendly drink option.
